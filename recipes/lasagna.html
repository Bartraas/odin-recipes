Lasagna

Making lasagna involves preparing a rich meat sauce, mixing a creamy cheese filling, layering these components with flat pasta sheets, and baking the dish until bubbly and golden

Ingredients

  • Ricotta cheese: 15 oz whole-milk ricotta (cottage cheese also works as a substitute)
  • Mozzarella cheese: 2 to 3 lbs or cups shredded low-moisture mozzarella, divided
  • Parmesan or Pecorino Romano: ½ to ¾ cup freshly grated
  • Eggs: 1 to 2 large eggs to bind the ricotta mixture

Steps

  1. Cook the Pasta and MeatBoil the lasagna noodles in a large pot of salted water until al dente, then drain.Cook the ground beef, onion, and garlic in a skillet over medium heat until the meat is fully browned.Drain excess fat, stir in the pasta sauce, and let it simmer for a few minutes.

  2. Prepare the Cheese Mixture, Mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl until smooth.
